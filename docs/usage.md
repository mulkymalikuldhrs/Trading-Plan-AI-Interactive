# How to Use MULKY AI TRADING OS™

1.  **Log a Trade:**
    *   Open the app and go to the "Entry" page.
    *   Fill out all the details of your trade setup.
    *   Select your current mood from the dropdown.
    *   Click "Validate Setup".
    *   Review the AI's feedback before executing your trade.
2.  **Reflect on Your Actions:**
    *   If you override the AI's advice, a violation will be logged.
    *   Go to the "AI Chat" page to discuss the override with your AI coach.
3.  **Review Your Week:**
    *   Every Friday, you will receive a WhatsApp notification that your weekly summary is ready.
    *   Go to the "Dashboard" to see a full analysis of your performance, including your dominant emotions and biggest leaks.
