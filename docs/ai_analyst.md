# AI Trade Analyst System

The MULKY AI TRADING COMPANION OS™ now includes a sophisticated AI Trade Analyst capable of performing multi-faceted market analysis and generating autonomous trading signals.

## Core Capabilities

1.  **Data Synthesis:** The AI is no longer just a psychological coach; it's a powerful analyst. It ingests and synthesizes data from four key areas:
    *   **Technical Analysis:** Real-time indicators like RSI, MACD, and moving averages.
    *   **Fundamental Analysis:** The latest news headlines and their sentiment.
    *   **Economic Events:** High-impact events from the economic calendar.
    *   **Positional Analysis:** The net positioning of large speculators from the Commitment of Traders (COT) report.

2.  **Hedge Fund-Level Prompting:** We use a "Master Trade Analyst Prompt" that instructs the AI to think like a hedge fund analyst. It must weigh the evidence from all data sources, form a coherent thesis for each, and then synthesize them into a final, high-conviction bias.

3.  **Autonomous Signal Generation:**
    *   The system periodically scans the markets for trading opportunities based on the user's predefined trading plan (e.g., a list of preferred currency pairs).
    *   When the AI's analysis results in a high confidence score (e.g., 7/10 or higher), it will generate a precise, actionable trading signal, including Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit levels.

4.  **Instant Delivery:** These high-conviction signals are delivered instantly to the user via both the in-app chatbot and a proactive WhatsApp message, ensuring the user never misses a key opportunity.

## The Goal: An AI-Powered Analyst on Your Team

The purpose of this system is to provide the user with a tireless, data-driven, and unbiased trading analyst working for them 24/7. It handles the heavy lifting of data collection and analysis, allowing the user to focus on execution and managing their own psychology.
