# 🔮 The Forecast Engine™

The Forecast Engine™ is the most advanced module in the Dhaher Trading Plan AI. It elevates the system from a reactive journal to a proactive, predictive analysis tool.

## How It Works

1.  **Multi-Factor Analysis:** The engine doesn't just look at one aspect of the market. It synthesizes a wide range of data points for a holistic view:
    *   **Technical Structure:** Market structure, key levels, Fair Value Gaps (FVGs), etc.
    *   **Fundamental Data:** High-impact news events, CPI, GDP, and central bank policy shifts.
    *   **Sentiment Analysis:** Commitment of Traders (COT) data to see how institutions are positioned.
    *   **Correlations:** The relationship between the asset and other instruments like the DXY or major indices.

2.  **AI-Powered Probabilistic Forecasting:** All of this data is fed into a specialized GPT prompt. The AI's task is not to "predict the future" with certainty, but to **calculate the probability of a specific trading scenario playing out**. It then provides a clear, actionable forecast based on the highest-probability outcome.

3.  **Visualized Projections:** The forecast isn't just text. The `ForecastTab` includes a chart that visually projects the AI's anticipated price path, complete with transparent "future" candles and clearly marked SL/TP zones.

4.  **On-Demand & Automated:**
    *   **On-Demand:** Users can request a forecast for any asset at any time using the `/forecast` command in the app or on WhatsApp.
    *   **Automated:** The system can be configured to automatically generate and send forecasts for key assets at the beginning of each week or trading session.

## The Goal: From Reactive to Proactive

The Forecast Engine™ is designed to shift the trader's mindset from being *reactive* to the market to being *proactive*. By understanding the probabilities and seeing a clear, data-driven picture of the week ahead, the trader can plan their trades with more confidence and precision. It's the ultimate fusion of data, AI, and strategic foresight.
