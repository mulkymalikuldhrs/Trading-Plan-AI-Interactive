This document outlines the structure of the Google Sheet that will serve as the primary database for the MULKY AI TRADING OS™.

---

## Sheet 1: `Trading Plan`

**Purpose:** To define all valid trading strategies and their rules. This sheet acts as the "constitution" for the AI validator.

| Column A | Column B | Column C | Column D | Column E |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
