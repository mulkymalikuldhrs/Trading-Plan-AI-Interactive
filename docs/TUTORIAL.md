# 📚 The Dhaher Trading Plan AI™ - Ultimate User Guide

This guide provides a complete A-Z workflow for using the Dhaher Trading Plan AI™ as a daily tool for disciplined, intelligent trading.

---

### 🚀 **1. System Startup**

Before you begin, ensure the entire system is online:
1.  **Open the Web App:** Navigate to the deployed URL of the Flutter application.
2.  **Log In:** Access your main Dashboard.
3.  **System Check:**
    *   **Google Sheet API:** Ensure it's active.
    *   **LLM7 GPT API:** Test the chatbot with a simple prompt like "Hello".
    *   **WhatsApp Bot:** Make sure the Node.js server is running and your phone is connected.

---

### 📅 **2. The Pre-Market Ritual (Daily Morning)**

1.  **Market Intelligence:**
    *   Navigate to the **`🧠 Market Intel`** tab.
    *   Review the latest high-impact news, check the economic calendar, and see the AI's summary of the Commitment of Traders (COT) data.
    *   Click the **`🔍 AI Smart Summary`** button to get a synthesized outlook for the day (e.g., "USD: strong bias, EUR/USD → bearish, Gold → sideway").

2.  **AI-Powered Forecasting:**
    *   Navigate to the **`🔮 Forecast Center`** tab.
    *   Select your primary pair (e.g., `XAU/USD`), your preferred timeframe (`H4`), and a time window (`7 Days`).
    *   Click **`🔮 Forecast`**.
    *   Analyze the AI's projected price path, probability score, and the suggested zones for Entry, SL, and TP.

3.  **Mental & Strategic Prep:**
    *   Open the **`💬 AI Chat`** panel.
    *   Ask clarifying questions to prepare your mind:
        *   *"What should be my primary focus today?"*
        *   *"Is EUR/USD still a valid pair to watch based on the forecast?"*
        *   *"Give me a motivational quote to stay disciplined."*

---

### 🖊️ **3. Trade Execution: The Entry Protocol**

1.  **Plan Your Trade:**
    *   Navigate to the **`📋 Entry Planner`** tab.
    *   Fill out all fields: Pair, Direction, Entry, SL, TP, your Setup, your current Mood, and your Reason for taking the trade.

2.  **Confirm with Your Co-Pilot:**
    *   Click **`✅ Confirm with AI`**.
    *   The AI will validate your plan against its market data and your predefined rules. It might respond: *"Setup valid, but FVG has not been filled. Suggest waiting for one more M15 candle for confirmation."*
    *   **Never proceed on a red "Blocked" signal.** If the signal is yellow (e.g., your mood is "Anxious"), be extra cautious.
    *   If you choose to ignore the AI's warning, you must click **`⚠️ Override`**, and this action will be logged as a violation.

3.  **Visualize:**
    *   Review the live **TradingView chart** embedded at the bottom of the form to confirm the setup visually.

4.  **Submit:**
    *   If the AI agrees, click **`📤 Submit`**.
    *   **Automation Triggered:**
        *   Your trade plan is instantly saved to the Google Sheet.
        *   You receive a WhatsApp notification: *“📍 Setup for EUR/USD is locked in. Stay disciplined. I'm watching with you.”*

---

### 📈 **4. During Market Hours**

The system works for you in the background:
-   It monitors if your entry levels are hit.
-   When a trade is closed (SL or TP), it automatically logs the result in the **`📘 Journal`** tab.
-   Your **Dashboard** is updated in real-time: the Equity Curve moves, the Win/Loss Pie Chart adjusts, and your Mood Tracker logs the trade's outcome.

---

### 📓 **5. Post-Trade: The Journaling Ritual**

1.  **Open the Journal:**
    *   Navigate to the **`📘 Journal`** tab.
    *   Find the trade you just closed.
    *   Add your post-trade comments and, most importantly, your **Emotion After** the trade.

2.  **Get Instant Feedback:**
    *   The AI will automatically provide feedback on your journal entry, such as: *"You closed this trade too early out of anxiety, even though the plan was valid. Let's work on patience."* or *"Perfect execution. This is the discipline that builds careers."*

---

### 🔁 **6. The Review Cycle (Daily & Weekly)**

-   **Daily:** At the end of your trading day, you will receive an automated WhatsApp summary of your daily performance.
-   **Weekly:** Every Sunday evening, the AI will perform a deep-dive analysis of your week and send a full report to your WhatsApp, including:
    *   A summary of your P&L and win rate.
    *   Your most profitable (and least profitable) setups.
    *   Your most dominant emotions and how they correlated with your results.
    *   A specific, actionable challenge for the week ahead.

---

### 🔒 **7. The Emotional Protection System**

The OS is designed to protect you from yourself:
-   **3 Overrides:** The UI will enter "Cooldown Mode," blurring the entry form and activating the AI chatbot to encourage reflection.
-   **High Emotion:** If you log trades with "Angry" or "FOMO" moods, you'll get a gentle nudge on WhatsApp to take a break.
-   **5-Day Consistency Streak:** The UI will celebrate you with a "🔥 ON FIRE!" animation and a new badge.
-   **3 Consecutive Losses:** The bot will send a message: *“Three losses in a row. The market is telling you to take a break. Let’s reflect.”*

---

### 📱 **10. Full Control from WhatsApp**

You can run your entire analysis on the go:
-   `/forecast GOLD`
-   `/summary EURUSD`
-   `/setup today`
-   `/journal last`

The Dhaher Trading Plan AI is more than an app; it's a **system for building an elite trading mindset.** Follow the ritual, trust the process, and let the AI be your guide.
