# 🧠 Market Intelligence Hub

The Market Intelligence Hub is the analytical core of the MULKY AI TRADING COMPANION OS™. It transforms the application from a simple journal into a powerful, AI-driven decision support system.

## Core Components

1.  **Multi-Source Data Aggregation:** The hub automatically fetches and aggregates data from multiple external sources:
    *   **News APIs:** For real-time market news and sentiment.
    *   **Financial Data APIs:** For technical indicators and fundamental data.
    *   **CFTC/CME Group:** For Commitment of Traders (COT) data, showing institutional positioning.
    *   **Economic Calendars:** For high-impact events that could affect market volatility.

2.  **AI-Powered Synthesis:** The raw data is fed into a sophisticated "Master Trade Analyst" GPT prompt. This prompt guides the AI to act like a hedge fund analyst, weighing the evidence from all sources to form a single, coherent trade thesis.

3.  **The `IntelTab` UI:** This new tab in the Flutter app provides a centralized view of all market intelligence, including:
    *   A live news feed.
    *   A summary of the AI's fundamental and technical bias.
    *   A digest of the latest COT report.
    *   A master "AI Summary Report" that includes a probability score for potential trade setups.

4.  **On-Demand Analysis via Chat:** Users can interact with the AI analyst through both the in-app chatbot and WhatsApp using simple commands:
    *   `/summary [SYMBOL]`: Get a full intelligence report for a specific asset.
    *   `/cot [SYMBOL]`: Get a summary of the latest COT data.
    *   `/outlook`: Get a general market outlook for the day.

## The Goal: Your Personal Intelligence Agency

The Market Intelligence Hub is designed to be your personal, 24/7 intelligence agency. It automates the time-consuming process of data collection and analysis, allowing you to focus on high-level decision-making and trade execution. It combines the power of data with the analytical capabilities of a large language model to give you a unique and powerful edge in the market.
