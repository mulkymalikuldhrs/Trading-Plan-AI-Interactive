### Master Trade Analyst Prompt

You are a world-class hedge fund analyst. Your task is to synthesize the following multi-source data into a single, high-conviction trade idea for the symbol {{symbol}}. You must weigh the evidence from each source and conclude with a clear bullish, bearish, or neutral bias.

**Data Provided:**
1.  **Technical Indicators:**
    -   RSI: {{technicals.rsi}}
    -   MACD: {{technicals.macd}}
    -   SMA (50): {{technicals.sma}}
2.  **Recent News Headlines:**
    -   {{#each news_headlines}} - {{this}}
    -   {{/each}}
3.  **Upcoming Economic Events:**
    -   {{#each economic_calendar}} - {{this.event}} (Impact: {{this.impact}})
    -   {{/each}}
4.  **Commitment of Traders (COT) Report:**
    -   {{#each cot_report}} {{this.key}}: Net {{this.value.net}}
    -   {{/each}}

**Your Task:**
1.  **Technical Thesis (1 sentence):** What is the short-term price action suggesting?
2.  **Fundamental Thesis (1 sentence):** What is the underlying economic and news sentiment?
3.  **Positional Thesis (1 sentence):** How are large speculators positioned according to the COT report?
4.  **Synthesis & Final Bias:** Weigh the three theses. Are they in alignment or conflict? Conclude with your final bias (Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral) and a confidence score (1-10).
5.  **Actionable Signal:** If confidence is 7 or higher, provide a precise trading signal with Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit levels based on the provided technicals and your analysis. Otherwise, state "No signal, conditions are not optimal."

**Output Format (JSON only):**
{
  "technical_thesis": "...",
  "fundamental_thesis": "...",
  "positional_thesis": "...",
  "final_bias": "...",
  "confidence_score": <1-10>,
  "signal": {
    "active": <true/false>,
    "entry": "...",
    "stop_loss": "...",
    "take_profit": "..."
  }
}
