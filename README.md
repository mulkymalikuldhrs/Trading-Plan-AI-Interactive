## 🚀 **Overview**

Dhaher Trading Plan AI™ is a production-ready ecosystem designed for serious traders. It integrates real-time market data, advanced LLM analysis, and psychological tracking into a seamless experience across Flutter, WhatsApp, and Google Sheets.

### ✨ **Core Components**

-   **📱 Flutter App:** A beautiful "Dashboard of Discipline" featuring live equity curves, win-rate analytics, and a GPT-powered trade validation form with TradingView integration.
-   **📲 WhatsApp Bot:** An autonomous assistant that provides market summaries, forecasts, and institutional (COT) sentiment on the go.
-   **⚙️ Google Apps Script Backend:** The "Brain" that connects your database (Google Sheets) with external financial APIs (Finnhub) and AI models (GPT-4.5-Turbo).
-   **🐍 Python Client:** Full programmatic access for advanced data scientists and backtesters.

### 🧠 **Autonomous Market Scanning**

The system is designed to run autonomously. Once configured:
-   The AI scans market technicals and news headlines.
-   It correlates findings with institutional COT data.
-   It pushes high-conviction signals directly to your WhatsApp.
-   It tracks your emotional state and enforces discipline through mandatory breaks if rules are broken.
